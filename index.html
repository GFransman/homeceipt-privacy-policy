Privacy Policy for Sliptag

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Sliptag is a household spending app. You photograph a shopping receipt, the app reads the items off it, sorts them into categories, and lets you record who in your household each item was for. Members of your household can share one household and see the same records.

This policy explains what we collect, what we cannot see, and what choices you have. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

Who we are. Sliptag is operated by Pytbyte Web and App Development (Pty) Ltd, Cape Town, South Africa. Contact: garth.fransman@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Receipt photos are never stored. They are processed and discarded.
  • Item names and shop names are encrypted on your device before they are saved. We cannot read them.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. The app is funded by scan packs.
  • You can delete your data at any time.

What we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and, if your Google account provides it, your name. This is used to identify your account, connect you to your household, and count your monthly scans. We do not receive your Google password.

Household information. The household name, the names or labels you create for household members (for example "Mom", "Dad", "Liam"), and which signed-in accounts belong to that household.

Spending records. For each receipt you save: the amount of each item, its category, the household member it was assigned to, and the date. Item names and shop names are also saved, but only in encrypted form (see "What we cannot see" below).

Usage information. The number of scans you have used in the current month, and basic technical information such as error reports and app version. This is used to enforce free scan limits and to fix problems.

Purchase information. If you buy a scan pack, the purchase is processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made and the number of scans to credit. We never receive or store your card details.

We do not collect your location, contacts, messages, calendar, health data, or the contents of your device's photo library beyond the single image you choose to scan.

What we cannot see

Item names and shop names are encrypted on your device using a key that is created on your phone and shared only with the household members you invite. That key is never sent to us and never stored on our servers.

This means that when we look at the data we hold, we see records like "Personal care — R89 — 14 August — member 3". We cannot see what the item was or which shop it came from.

If every device in your household is lost and you have not saved your household key, that encrypted information cannot be recovered — not by you and not by us. This is a deliberate consequence of the design. The app provides a "Back up household key" option for this reason.

Receipt photos and AI processing

When you scan a receipt, the photo is sent to Anthropic's API, which reads the items and prices from the image and returns them as text. This is the only purpose the image is used for.

  • The photo is held in memory only for the duration of that request and is then discarded.
  • The photo is not saved to our servers, our logs, or any database.
  • Anthropic does not use data submitted through its API to train its models.
  • Information sent alongside the image describes household members only by general role — for example "the adult woman" or "the 12-year-old boy" — never by the names you have entered.

If you would prefer not to use AI processing at all, you can enter items manually. Manual entry is unlimited, free, and sends nothing to any AI service.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising or marketing purposes.

We share information only with the service providers necessary to operate the app:

Google (Firebase) Sign-in, database, syncing between household devices Account identifiers and your spending records, with item and shop names encrypted
Anthropic Reading items off a scanned receipt The receipt image and role descriptions, retained only for the duration of the request

Google Play Processing purchases Purchase details handled entirely by Google

We may also disclose information if we are legally required to do so, but we can only disclose what we actually hold — which does not include readable item or shop names.

Other members of your household

Sliptag is designed for shared use. Anyone you invite into your household can see the records in that household, including item names, amounts, categories and who each item was assigned to.

Please only invite people you intend to share this information with. Anyone who receives your household invite also receives the household key.

If you want to record something without its name being visible to other members, use the private item option, which stores only the category and the amount and discards the item name entirely.

Children

Sliptag is intended for adults. Children can be recorded as household members whose items are tracked, but this does not require an account for them, and we do not knowingly collect information directly from children under 13 (or under 18 where local law defines that as a child). If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep information

Spending records are kept until you delete them or delete your account. Scan counts are kept for the current and previous month. Error reports are kept for up to 90 days.

Your rights and choices

You can:

  • See your data — everything the app holds about your household is visible in the app itself.
  • Delete individual records — delete any item or receipt at any time.
  • Delete your account and data — from Settings, or by emailing garth.fransman@gmail.com. We will action deletion requests within 30 days.
  • Leave a household — your account is removed from that household's records.
  • Export or object — contact us and we will help.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (South Africa), the GDPR (Europe and the UK), or similar laws — including the right to access, correct, restrict, or object to our processing of your information, and the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. In South Africa, that is the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

Our lawful basis for processing is the performance of our agreement with you (providing the app) and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working.

Where information is stored

Our service providers process and store information on servers that may be located outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. Where information is transferred internationally, we rely on the safeguards offered by those providers, including standard contractual clauses.

Security

Item and shop names are encrypted on your device before transmission. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Our API keys are held server-side and are never included in the app. Our servers do not log item names, shop names, or receipt images.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your information, we will notify you and the relevant regulator as required by law.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
