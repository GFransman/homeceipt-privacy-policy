Sliptag — Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: 14 August 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Sliptag account and data — including if you no longer have the app installed. Sliptag is operated by Pytbyte Web and App Development (Pty) Ltd, Cape Town, South Africa. For our full privacy policy, see Sliptag's Privacy Policy.

How to request deletion

Option 1: In the app

Open Sliptag, go to Settings → Delete account and data, and confirm. This works whether or not your household has other members.

Option 2: By email

Don't have the app installed anymore? Email garth.fransman@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my Sliptag data", sent from the same email address you signed in with (or naming it in the email). We'll confirm by reply once it's done.

Requests are processed within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (Google sign-in link, email address on file)
  • Your scan usage history and purchase-credit records
  • Your membership in any household — you're removed from its member list
  • Any spending records you're the sole owner of, in households where you're the only member

What doesn't get deleted automatically

Sliptag is built for shared household use, so a couple of things work differently from a single-user app:

  • Shared household records. If you're one of several members in a household, deleting your account removes you from it, but the household's shared spending records (which item and shop names are already encrypted, per our privacy policy) remain for the other members, since they belong to the household, not to you individually. If you want the entire household and all its records deleted, say so explicitly in your request and confirm you're doing so on behalf of every remaining member, or ask each member to delete their own account until none remain.
  • Purchase records. Google Play purchase records may be retained by Google as required for accounting, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes, independent of this request. We don't control that retention — see Google Play's own policies.
  • Error/technical logs already scheduled for automatic deletion (kept a maximum of 90 days regardless) may take up to that long to fully age out, even after your account is deleted.
